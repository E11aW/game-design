# Project Proposal

## Elevator Pitch
Are you stressed? Overworked? Or just need an escape from reality? Then leave the boring real world you can’t control behind you and come explore the world of Cipher of the Deep, one you can control! Full of adventure, combat, and treasure: Cipher of the Deep allows users to embark on their own journey where they choose who they want to be and what they will accomplish. Choose your class, attacks, and paths—becoming the type of adventurer YOU want to be. Endless opportunities and discoveries await you: go explore!
## Game Synopsis
In Cipher of the Deep players start off by choosing a class: either a fighter, mage, or rogue. This determines their playstyle and the type of loot players will find in chests, getting armor and weapons that match their class. Players will traverse further into the dungeon to try and defeat the final boss, an evil mage who lured them into the dungeon. Enemies slain grant the player experience to level up and gain new skills and attacks, allowing them to grow stronger as they explore deeper levels. Every 5 levels or so the player will have to defeat a boss to progress that will test their strength and skills.
### Objective
The main objective is to explore the dungeon: defeating enemies and progressing to further levels.
### Mechanics
The player navigates the dungeon, interacting with enemies or chests to open up sub-screens. Chests give players armor, weapons, and items. Combat will be turn-based, allowing users to think through their actions on whether they want to attack, run, or use an item. Defeating enemies gives the player experience they can use to level up and gain new skills, and advance to the next floor of the dungeon. Every 5 or so floors there will be special boss levels to overcome.
